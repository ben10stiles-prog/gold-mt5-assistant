TESTING RULES

The test uses the existing GD-MTF-1.0 signal rules without tuning. Entry is the signal candle close; exits begin on the next candle. If stop and target are both touched in one candle, the stop is counted first. Spread, commission, slippage and swap are excluded, so results are optimistic and are not live-trading performance.

Use a large, representative dataset and keep a separate out-of-sample period. A few days of data is not enough to establish an edge.
